🌍 Welcome to Eco Living

Eco Living is all about making small lifestyle changes that help protect our planet. Every little action counts toward creating a cleaner and healthier environment.

♻️ Simple Ways to Go Green

  • Carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Use cloth bags instead of plastic bags.
  • Save electricity by switching off unused lights.
  • Reduce food waste and compost organic waste.
  • Use public transport whenever possible.
  • Plant trees and take care of green spaces.
